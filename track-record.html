Track Record

Transparency matters. Below is a selection of past views and how they evolved. This section will grow as more published calls are documented.

Directionally correct Published: Mar 2025

Labour market cooling without a sharp rise in unemployment

Viewed that the US labour market would continue to rebalance through slower hiring and moderating wage growth rather than large-scale layoffs. Subsequent data largely supported this path into late 2025.

Partially correct Published: Nov 2024

Term premia likely to rise as fiscal concerns gain attention

Anticipated upward pressure on long-end yields from term premia. The move materialised later and was more volatile than initially expected; the directional thesis held but timing and magnitude required adjustment.

Ongoing Published: Jun 2026

Global growth more resilient than consensus recession models implied

Current working view. Early evidence from activity data and corporate indicators has been supportive, but the assessment remains open and will be updated as new information arrives.

Note: Track records are inherently selective and forward-looking judgments can be wrong. This page exists to encourage accountability, not to claim consistent outperformance. Past views are not a guarantee of future accuracy.
