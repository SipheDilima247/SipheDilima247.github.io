IPOs that will change the game

October 01, 2026 • 8 min read

If you are back again reading our reports, you will sure notice a bias; we are bullish on Energy, Pharma and Finance. With that being said; most of the IPOs that will be mentioned here are either in one of these industries or cruising safely between the 3. We wont repeat the consensus on our last report but if you want to know why we hold this bias then we encourage you to go back to our first report here

When it comes to IPOs in general, all the good names that ended up being house-hold or Wall Street giants have majorly followed the same type of pattern/trend. Now it took us a while to come to this conclusion but the rationale behind it is quite amazing; The IPOs usually come priced in at double digit bid prices eg $15-$90, this IPO rides this range for a while BUT it will certainly reverse to half of the IPO bid or even less. At this point in time the only recovery the IPO can make is if it produces and sells enough product to keep the stock floating and even better if they can get government contracts or score service provider contracts with Big brands. This move is what separates a real company selling real products and/or services to companies trying to make a quick buck through going IPO. And this is exactly what makes you and I a quick buck through IPOs.

There are a lot of ways to know when which company goes IPO. But our preferred mode is through YahooFinance, now this is not an endorsement or anything like that, we just like how simple and practical it is. https://finance.yahoo.com/calendar/ipo, scroll through to find the date and find which IPOs are going live on that day and do your research on the names and Bob is your uncle.

IPOs

Lets get into the names then, please note that these prices are true as of 1 October 2026:

Standard Nuclear

An independent American nuclear fuel company specializing in the industrial-scale manufacturing of advanced TRISO nuclear fuel. TRISO (tri-structural isotropic) particle fuel is an advanced, highly robust form of micro-particle nuclear fuel designed to act as its own independent containment system. In America, there are 3 companies that currently produce/manufacture TRISO: BWX Technologies, TRISO-X & Standard Nuclear. Based in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, Standard Nuclear is the nation's only independent developer of TRISO fuel. The company has already commenced advanced High-Assay Low-Enriched Uranium (HALEU) TRISO production at its pilot facilities. To scale to massive commercial quantities, Standard Nuclear entered into a joint venture with global nuclear giant Framatome and secured major funding to expand annual TRISO production to over two metric tons https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/us-companies-selected-for-advanced-fuel-production.

Currently going for $11.81 a share it wouldn't be a bad price range to acquire given it IPOed at $13.52. Although our consensus tells us to wait for a drop to 50% of its IPO price before we get in, Standard Nuclear did reverse to about $7.03 so that would've been the perfect price. Although we do not see it going back it those levels, optimism never hurt nobody. However, if you do enter now and it dips to the 50% IPO levels, then all you have to do is add more to your bag and ride the wave.

Ionic Digital Inc

Ionic Digital Inc. is a digital infrastructure and Bitcoin mining company that trades on the Nasdaq under the ticker symbol IOND. Ionic Digital builds and leases high-performance digital infrastructure for artificial intelligence (AI) and high-performance computing (HPC) workloads and those it does not lease it uses to mine Bitcoin. This one is a wildcard but we hold a hard consensus that it is a diamond in the rough and we predict that it will eventually be bought up by a larger name(XAI, SpaceX, Anthropic, OpenAI, etc.) if not, we see them going into Quantum Computing IF/WHEN their revenue streams go as planned.

Currently going for $80.33 a share, it is currently on a very good bull run. We are still waiting for a reversal to about $25-$30 since it IPOed at exactly $50. Again; if you entered, and the bull run reverses, do not get frustrated just buy some more on the way down because this company will eventually weather all and any storms coming its way.

Jersey Mike’s Subs Inc., is an American multinational submarine sandwich chain headquartered in Manasquan, New Jersey. The Jersey Mike's franchise has about 3,000 locations, with locations in the United States, Canada, and elsewhere. According to us, this might as well be the next Subway or Starbucks in terms of exposure and cult-like following.

Currently going for $16.82, it is well on its way to the 50% IPO price of $10.50 as it IPOed at exactly $21.

Latigo Biotherapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company based in Thousand Oaks, California, focused on developing innovative, non-opioid pain medicines. Advasa Holdings, Inc. is a financial technology holding company headquartered in Minato, Tokyo, Japan, operating globally with a primary focus on patented Earned Wage Access (EWA) solutions. Earned Wage Access (EWA) software lets employees draw accrued wages before payday to avoid high-cost debt https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/payments-unbound/volume-3/ewa-income-access-for-all. Electra Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company that develops precision medicines targeting signal regulatory protein (SIRP) biology to selectively deplete disease-driving immune cells in immunology and cancer.
