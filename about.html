About Nexus Consensus

Nexus Consensus is an independent research platform focused on economic and financial markets analysis.

The goal is simple: take high-quality institutional research — from the IMF, BIS, major central banks, and leading banks such as Standard Chartered — and combine it with independent interpretation and community perspectives. The result is a clearer, more complete view of the forces shaping markets and the economy.

What we publish

  • Macro and market analysis grounded in data and institutional research
  • Clear framing of competing narratives and where the evidence points
  • Regular notes on labour markets, growth, inflation, policy, and financial conditions
  • Selected community insights that add signal

How we work

Every major piece is built on primary sources and transparent reasoning. We do not claim perfect foresight. What we aim for is intellectual honesty, clear communication, and a willingness to update views when the data change.

Community

Research improves when it is stress-tested. The Community section exists so readers can share alternative readings, additional data points, or constructive challenges. High-quality contributions are reviewed and may be featured.
